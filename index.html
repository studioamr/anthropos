Anthropos I · Panama

Luxury residences
and resorts.

Stripped classicism for the tropics. Travertine, bronze, shade, and water, laid out with the discipline of the Italian Rationalists.

Our first residential project, Anthropos I, is planned for Panama's Pacific coast. Anthropos designs homes, hotels, and clubs for its members, built to the standard of the world's finest hotels and made to outlast us.

Concept studies for the Anthropos resort and residences: main pool, sculpture court, grand salon, spa, residence courtyard, beach boardwalk, and arrival court.

Phase I

Beginning on the
Azuero Coast.

A waterfront neighborhood of private residences, a resort, and rooms for art and music.

We are studying sites on the Pacific shore of the Azuero Peninsula, five hours from Panama City and its international hub. Phase I is a neighborhood of homes and shared spaces for residents, fellows, and guests. The first residents will arrive in existing buildings near the site while the neighborhood takes shape around them.

An autonomous civic entity

Superintelligence for
collective action

We are building toward an autonomous republic that uses superintelligence to understand its members, coordinate shared goals, and improve with every campaign. It is bound by a written charter, overseen by human citizens who vote, and funded by a common budget of compute.

Life in Anthropos.

Turning superintelligence wealth into culture.

A home for a new generation of founders, artists, musicians, scholars, and athletes. The people building the superintelligence economy want a culture worth belonging to. We are gathering them to turn new wealth into art, music, film, sport, nightlife, and the rooms where all of it happens.
