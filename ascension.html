Anthropos

A coastal city for more than one hundred thousand people: seven districts, a river, a bay and a great park. Drag to look around and tap any place to explore it.

System

—

    The loop

    One closed circle,
    from the sun back to the soil.

    1. ISunA solar field and a wind ridge make more power than the city uses.
    2. IIPowerA microgrid and batteries carry the afternoon into the night.
    3. IIIWaterThe sea and the rain become drinking water, and every litre is reused.
    4. IVFoodGreenhouses, fields and the bay feed the city within sight.
    5. VWasteNothing leaves: scraps become gas, materials become buildings.
    6. VISoilCompost returns to the fields, and the circle begins again.
    Anthropos I · Residences

    Residences and resorts built to last centuries.

    Modern architecture with classical discipline. Stone, bronze, timber and water.

    Our first project is a coastal neighbourhood of private residences, a resort and members’ clubs, built to the standard of the world’s finest hotels and designed to outlive their builders. Every home has a garden, a view of the sea and the forum within a short walk.

    A self-governing city

    Superintelligence for collective action.

    Anthropos uses superintelligence to understand its citizens, coordinate shared goals and improve with every campaign. It proposes; people decide. Everything it does is bound by a written charter and human oversight.

    Life in practice

    Turning new wealth into culture.

    A home for a new generation of founders, artists, musicians and designers.

    The people building the superintelligence economy want a culture to belong to. Anthropos brings them together to turn that wealth into art, music, film, food and nightlife, and into the rooms where all of it happens.

    Development

    Real estate in the age of superintelligence.

    As superintelligence creates more wealth, the best places to live stay scarce.

    We believe that makes exceptional land more valuable over time. Anthropos plans to acquire land and develop homes, neighbourhoods and cities around a shared culture.

    Government partnerships

    Bring the superintelligence economy to your country.

    Partner with Anthropos to attract foreign investment, superintelligence companies and the people shaping technology and culture.

    Our ambition is to turn the wealth of this new economy into new companies, new jobs and places where people want to live, under the laws of every country that hosts us.
