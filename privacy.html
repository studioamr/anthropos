Privacy and terms.

Last updated October 16, 2026. This notice explains what Anthropos collects through this site and why. It will be reviewed by counsel before membership opens.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, email, city, the work you do, your answers about the home and city you want, and any friends’ emails you choose to share. The site uses no advertising trackers.

How we use it

To review applications, to count commitments in aggregate, to write to you about the campaign, and to plan the city. Individual answers are never published. Aggregates (for example, “58% want a home on the water”) may be.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export, or delete your data at any time by writing to the founding office. Friends whose emails you share will receive at most one invitation, and are never added to a list without their consent.

Terms

Not an offer of securities. Nothing on this site is an offer to sell, or a solicitation to buy, any security, token, or investment. Membership carries no right to profits or financial return.

Not an offer of real estate. Renderings and plans are concept studies. No land has been acquired, and no home is offered for sale on this site. Any future sale will be made under the laws of the host country, by licensed parties, with full disclosures.

A commitment is a statement of intent, not a deposit, contract, or reservation, and may be withdrawn at any time.

Host countries. Anthropos acts only under the laws of the countries where it operates and does not claim sovereignty over any territory.

Images. Architectural and lifestyle images on this site are concept renderings created for Anthropos. They do not depict existing buildings, events, or members.
