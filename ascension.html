A1 The City

Polis

One hundred thousand people in four districts around a single forum. Everything you need daily is a fifteen-minute walk away. The city produces its own power, water and most of its food, and returns what it uses to the land.

  • 01Residents (target)100,000
  • 02Districts4
  • 03Walk to the forum≤ 15 min
  • 04Cars inside the ring0
A2 Energy

The sun pays the floor.

A solar field north of the city and wind turbines on the ridge feed a city microgrid. Batteries store the afternoon for the night. Every roof is a generator. Power is infrastructure, like streets: built once, shared by everyone.

  • 01Solar field320 MW
  • 02Wind on the ridge60 MW
  • 03Battery storage1.2 GWh
  • 04Yearly demand covered110%

Design targets, sized for ~6 MWh per resident per year in a tropical site.

A3 Water

From the sea, back to the soil.

A solar-powered desalination plant on the shore fills the reservoir. Roofs and squares capture the rain. Every litre used in homes is treated and sent back through the canals to irrigate farms and gardens. Nothing is pumped from aquifers.

  • 01Daily supply15,000 m³
  • 02Per resident150 L/day
  • 03Wastewater reused100%
  • 04Rain capturedAll roofs
A4 Food

Grown within sight.

Greenhouses and terraced fields on the east edge grow vegetables, fruit and grains with recycled water and the city's own compost. Fish farms work the bay. Fresh food travels a few kilometres, not a few thousand.

  • 01Greenhouses40 ha
  • 02Terraced fields600 ha
  • 03Fresh produce grown locallyMost
  • 04IrrigationRecycled
B1 The Loop

Nothing leaves.

Waste is sorted where it is made. Food scraps and sewage sludge go to the biodigester, which makes gas for the kitchens and compost for the fields. Glass, metal and stone are reused in construction. The goal is a city without a landfill.

  • 01Organic waste→ Biogas + compost
  • 02Materials→ Rebuilt
  • 03Landfill0
B2 Movement

A city you walk.

Cars stop at the edge. Inside, an electric tram runs a ring around the districts every four minutes, and two straight avenues cross at the forum. Streets are for people, shade and trees.

  • 01Tram frequency4 min
  • 02Ring length9 km
  • 03ParkingEdge only
B3 Homes

Stone, shade and courtyards.

Low, dense buildings of local stone with green roofs and inner courtyards. Thick walls, deep shade and the sea breeze cool them before any machine does. Built to stand for centuries, not decades.

  • 01Height3–6 floors
  • 02RoofsGreen + solar
  • 03Design life500 years
B4 The Forum

Where life after work happens.

The center of the city: the assembly, the baths, the theater, the library and the market around one great square. The city is governed by a written charter, and its citizens vote on it here.

  • 01Assembly · Baths · Theater1 square
  • 02GovernanceCharter
  • 03CitizenshipChosen
