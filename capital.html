04 · Tokenization

How a city is
owned in pieces.

Seven steps, from a coin to a coastline.

01

The piece

The city is owned by one company. Its equity is divided into 10,000,000 digital units. One token is one piece of the company that owns the land, the buildings and the income.

02

The vehicle

A company built only for Anthropos I: Mexican for the land, with a Delaware parent for international investors. One owner, one set of books, audited every year.

03

The trust

The land is held by a Mexican bank in a fideicomiso, as the law requires near the coast. Nobody can sell or pledge it without the approvals the documents set.

04

The land

The seed round secures an option on the land and the permits. The land round buys it at a known price. Money waits in escrow until each step is proven.

05

The city

Funds are released by milestones: infrastructure, first homes, the resort, the academy. Every release is visible to every holder.

06

The return

Homes are sold and rented, and the resort and club operate. What the company earns, after costs and taxes, flows back to holders in proportion.

07

The market

After the legal holding period, tokens can be transferred between verified investors on a regulated venue. A piece of a city, as auditable as a file.
