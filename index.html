Luxury residences
and resorts.

Stripped classicism for the present day. Stone, bronze, and water, set out with the discipline of the Italian Rationalists. Our first residential project, Anthropos I, is in Panama. Anthropos designs homes, hotels, and clubs for its members, built to the standard of the world’s finest hotels and made to last.

05 · Phase I

Beginning on the
Azuero Coast.

A waterfront neighborhood of private residences, a resort, and spaces for art and music. We are developing the first phase of Anthropos Panama on the Pacific shore of the Azuero Peninsula, building the homes and shared spaces for residents, fellows, and guests. The first residents will move into existing buildings as the neighborhood develops around them.

The Azuero coast. The first residents will live nearby while the Anthropos neighborhood takes shape around them.

09 · Anthropos Development

Real estate for the superintelligence age.

As superintelligence creates more wealth, the best places to live will remain scarce. We believe that will make exceptional land more valuable. Anthropos plans to acquire land and develop homes, neighborhoods, and cities around a shared culture.
