The location

Why Panama.

Two oceans. A dollar economy. A hub of the Americas, a few hours’ flight from much of the hemisphere.

We looked across the Americas and the Mediterranean for a place to build. Panama has the landscape, the openness to newcomers, and the space. On the Pacific shore of the Azuero Peninsula we can live on the water, a short flight or a long drive from the capital, with room to make something of our own.

The coast

Long beaches, dry tropical forest, and hills that fall to the sea, under a long dry season.

The connections

Panama City’s airport links the hemisphere and Europe; the Canal links the world.

The country

A constitutional democracy that uses the US dollar and has long welcomed foreign residents.

The setting

Fishing towns, surf beaches, and colonial villages nearby, with room for a new neighborhood to grow.

Phase I

Beginning on the
Azuero Coast.

A waterfront neighborhood of private residences, a resort, and rooms for art and music.

Phase I is a neighborhood of homes and shared spaces for residents, fellows, and guests. We are studying sites now, and will choose with the members who commit to move. The first residents will arrive in existing buildings near the site while the neighborhood takes shape around them.

Lyceon Residences

Residences
and resort.

Stone, bronze, and gardens opening onto the water.

Our designs draw on the Italian Rationalists and on the courtyard houses of the tropics: generous proportions, colonnades, deep shade, and quiet courts. A long pool, a salon, a walk down to the beach. Private homes alongside a resort, for a few days or much longer.

Concept studies for the Lyceon resort and residences: main pool, sculpture court, grand salon, spa, residence courtyard, beach boardwalk, arrival court, the water walk, and the academy on the hill.

Culture

Life in Lyceon.

Music, art, dinners, sport, and late nights will be part of Lyceon from the first day. On the Azuero Coast we are planning studios, galleries, and venues into the neighborhood from the beginning, not added later.

Shared spaces

Around the neighborhood.

Studios, gardens, places to eat, and a waterfront to spend the afternoon. Planned into the neighborhood for residents and fellows alike.
