Manifest

For ten thousand years we worked to survive. Machines now do most of that work, and nobody built a place for what comes after. Anthropos is that place: a new city where no one has to work to live.

A1

Abundance1 comes first. Energy, housing and food are designed as infrastructure, so the floor of life is paid before anyone wakes up.

A2

Citizenship2 is chosen, not inherited. You apply, you contribute, you belong. A city built by the people who want to live in it.

B1

The forum3 returns. Squares, baths, theaters and workshops replace the commute, the cubicle and the scroll.

B2

Built to last4. Stone, light and water. Architecture meant to still be standing in five hundred years.

Anthropos

Anthropos Cities is a startup building the first city designed for life after labor. Not a resort, not a gated suburb: a working city with its own charter, its own culture and room for one hundred thousand people who chose to be there.

A:

Why now.

1

Automation is making the old bargain, trade your hours for your survival, optional for the first time in history. Countries are competing for talent with special economic zones, charter cities and new residency programs. The tools to design, finance and govern a new city exist today. What is missing is a team willing to build one from zero.

B:

How it gets built.

2

In sequence. First a community of founding citizens and a founding round. Then the land, negotiated with a host government. Then a first district of homes, the forum and the infrastructure that pays the floor of life. Every phase is unlocked by the one before it, and every number we publish is one we can defend.
